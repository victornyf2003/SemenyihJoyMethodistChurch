Mission & Vision

Who we are, and what we're here to do

We're a healthy church that soars like an eagle — rooted in worship, growing in fellowship, and reaching out in service.

Our Mission

Semenyih Joy Methodist Church exists to provide a place where children and members worship the Lord and learn God's Word through games, singing, and small-group fellowship. We want every visitor to feel at home from their very first Sunday with us.

Our Vision

To be a healthy, multicultural congregation that soars like an eagle — anchored in faith, lifting one another up, and carrying the Gospel into our Semenyih community and beyond.

What We Value

Worship

Christ-centred Sunday services and Holy Communion that welcome every generation.

Fellowship

Small groups and community life where members grow together, not alone.

Service

Reaching out to Semenyih and beyond with practical care and God's Word.
